# Research Context Note

## 1. Starting Point

The starting point is a small state-description problem. Suppose two configurations are treated as dynamically equivalent under a one-drive-period shift. They may belong to the same declared equivalence class, yet they are still two records. They may occupy different temporal phase positions. They may also be assigned to different domains. A boundary between such domains may need to be represented somehow, but the right representation is not obvious.

The demonstrator isolates exactly that state-description problem. It does not start from gravitation, spacetime, or a broad theory claim. It starts with three synthetic records: `DTC_A`, `DTC_B`, and `BOUNDARY_AB`. `DTC_A` and `DTC_B` share the declared dynamic equivalence class `DTC_EQ_CLASS_01`. They remain distinct records. Their phase positions are `0T` and `1T`, and their domains are `DOMAIN_A` and `DOMAIN_B`. `BOUNDARY_AB` is a separate boundary record whose representation status remains open.

## 2. The Wider QSB Research Context

QSB is an independent, data-driven, relational and reproducible research framework. In its wider form it investigates whether disciplined descriptions of state, correlation and identity can form a controlled interface between quantum-mechanical descriptions and later geometric interpretation. That wider programme is not being submitted to the Gurevich group here.

The present material uses only a small method-level slice of that work. It asks whether a compact relational representation can keep identity, equivalence, phase position and boundary status apart without smuggling in a stronger physical conclusion. The answer may be that the representation is useful, trivial, misleading, underspecified, or simply not the right language for the problem. That is the level at which the material is meant to be read.

## 3. Why This Small Demonstrator Was Isolated

The demonstrator was isolated to avoid dragging a broad research programme into a first technical question. A large framework can easily make a simple issue look heavier than it is. The small package therefore removes unrelated QSB material and keeps only one synthetic dataset, a compact table, a figure and a technical note.

This also reduces the burden on the reader. The question is not whether QSB is correct. The question is whether, in a situation involving phase-shifted equivalent configurations and long-lived boundaries, it is methodologically clean to separate record identity, declared dynamic equivalence, temporal phase position, domain membership and boundary representation.

## 4. Methodological Working Style

The working style is deliberately traceable. Canonical records are kept separate from display labels. Explicit schemas, controlled values and documented transformation rules define the record layer, while validation checks and reproducible outputs make each derivation inspectable. Data, logic and interpretation remain distinct.

Negative controls and limitations are documented rather than hidden. A readable figure or an internally consistent result is not treated as evidence of physical adequacy. The point is not polish; the point is to leave a trace that can be checked without turning formal consistency into a physical claim.

## 5. What Is Established

The demonstrator exists. It contains exactly three records. The dynamic equivalence between `DTC_A` and `DTC_B` is declared. Their temporal phase positions are separate. Their domains are separate. The boundary representation is explicit but open. Internal consistency checks confirm that the three records, their declared relations and the visible outputs remain mutually consistent.

The established result is deliberately limited: there is a small, readable and internally consistent method demonstrator that carries three technical questions without changing the canonical records.

## 6. What Is Not Established

The limits are part of the material, not an afterthought. The demonstrator has no experimental derivation, is not a model of a real laser, makes no physical prediction and does not explain a mechanism. The separate boundary record remains a representation option, not a validated physical ontology.

A complete JSON Schema validation is not claimed where it has not been performed. QSB has not received independent scientific validation in this contact context, and the work is not presented as institutionally affiliated.

The representation is also not claimed to have universal validity. It is a constrained way to expose a question about identity, equivalence, phase position and boundary status.

The demonstrator should therefore not be read as evidence about the reported experiment, or about discrete time crystals in general. It is a constrained prompt for technical criticism.

## 7. Why the Gurevich Group Is Being Asked

The public research profile of the Gurevich group makes the group a plausible place to ask this narrow methodological question. The fit does not arise from any presumed connection between the group and QSB. The fit is methodological: nonlinear dynamics, time-delayed systems, optical systems, equivalent or shifted dynamical states, domains, boundary-like structures, local transitions and global order.

The recent public DTC context also overlaps with the concepts used in the demonstrator: shifted equivalent configurations, coexisting domains and long-lived boundaries. That public context motivates the question, but it does not make the synthetic demonstrator a model of the experiment.

## 8. Scope of the Requested Assessment

The requested assessment is limited to three technical questions:

1. Identity, equivalence and phase: Is it methodologically useful to distinguish record identity, dynamic equivalence and temporal phase position for two configurations related by a one-drive-period shift?
2. Boundary representation: What would be a minimal adequate representation for long-lived boundaries between coexisting equivalent configurations: a state label, a distinct boundary object, or another dynamical description?
3. Minimum physical information: What minimum state or observable information would be required for such a relational comparison to become physically meaningful rather than merely formally consistent?

A short answer that marks the distinction as useful, trivial, misleading or incomplete would already be enough. No collaboration, endorsement, supervision, institutional link or assessment of the full QSB programme is requested.
