# Figures

The `figures/` directory contains two different visual classes.

## Evidence-bearing scientific figures

These are generated from documented source data, model outputs, or validation results.

They may support scientific statements when their source, method, and caption are explicit.

Examples currently present include:

- `figure_gain_profile.png`
- `figure_mixed_sensitivity.png`
- run- or publication-specific plots and validation cards

## Conceptual and editorial illustrations

These are used as eye-catchers, section dividers, and reading pauses.

They help readers approach an abstract idea, but they are not simulations, observations, or scientific evidence.

Examples include:

- `figure_conceptual_bridge.png`
- publication eye-catchers and magazine-style overview graphics

Every public use should label the image role, for example:

> Conceptual illustration; not a numerical model output.

## Platform-specific visual language

### Formal journal paper

Primarily evidence figures, diagrams, tables, and equations in the journal’s standard style.

### Academia

Evidence figures plus selected editorial illustrations with science-magazine pacing and clear role labels.

### ESA OSIP

Technically elegant diagrams and concept visuals, less formal than a journal article and less magazine-like than the Academia version.

## Reuse and provenance

Before external republication, verify:

- source or generation method;
- relationship to a documented run;
- caption and claim role;
- file-specific licence or attribution requirements;
- whether the image is evidence-bearing or editorial.
