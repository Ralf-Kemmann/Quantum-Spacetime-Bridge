M-H1: Yoneya/Schild and LQG can be connected through the formal motif of area/geometry-like operator structures with spectral diagnostics.

M-H2: QSB/PBR pair-lag cells may be modeled as relational carriers whose class projectors define an area-like operator structure.

M-H3: The rank/PSD/eigenstructure of the QSB/PBR matrix may be treated as a geometry-like operator diagnostic.

M-H4: Structures dismissed as index, class, matrix, or discretization artifacts may be re-examined as construction-level operator signals.

All four are future design hypotheses, not claims.
