# QSB-META02 Final Result Note

## Befund

QSB-META02 creates a cross-mart key-mapping and transformation registry layer. The layer documents mapping fields, transformation rules, semantic relations, join statuses, seed mappings, candidate join examples, validation checks, and a copied metadata-catalog update path.

## Interpretation

The registry is infrastructure for later cross-mart integration. It separates technical joins from scientific joins and keeps unit, dimension, identity, lineage, evidence, review, and claim-boundary states explicit.

## Hypothese

Future cross-mart work can use META02 to compare CORRCORE01, CAUSALITY07, IDSPACE/CPNS, and later marts without relying on field-name similarity or label equality.

## Offene Luecke

Seed mappings remain conservative. Several mappings are pending, blocked, planned, or out of scope because unit, dimension, identity, or claim-boundary compatibility has not been validated.

## Claim Boundary

META02 does not claim QSB proof, Interface-layer establishment, demonstrated cross-mart convergence, physical validation, identity resolution, or evidence transfer by analogy. A blocked join is not negative evidence; it is an explicit metadata state.
