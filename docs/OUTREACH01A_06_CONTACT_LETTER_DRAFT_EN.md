Its purpose is bookkeeping clarity, not a physical claim.

What I would value is a limited technical reaction: whether this separation of record identity, dynamic equivalence, phase position and boundary representation is useful, trivial, misleading or incomplete for discussing such states.

I am seeking only a brief technical assessment of the representation itself; no broader commitment is implied. If the framing is outside your group’s interest, I will understand that completely.
