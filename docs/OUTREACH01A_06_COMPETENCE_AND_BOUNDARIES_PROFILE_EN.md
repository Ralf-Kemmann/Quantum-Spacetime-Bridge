# OUTREACH01A-06 Competence and Boundaries Profile

## 1. Scientific Background

I am a Diplom-Chemiker. My scientific background is rooted in inorganic chemistry, solid-state chemistry and a physical-chemical way of thinking: structure, measurable constraints, state descriptions, transformations and the difference between an interpretation and a result. This is not a claim of established expertise in laser physics or discrete time-crystal experiments. It is a statement of training and working orientation.

My chemistry background matters here because it brings a habit of separating composition, structure, state, phase, boundary and evidence level. I use that habit in the OUTREACH01A synthetic dynamical-state demonstrator, while keeping the physical limits explicit.

## 2. Computational and Data Background

My computational side is long-standing: software development, scientific data processing, data architecture, statistics, schema work, validation logic and reproducible pipelines. My practical emphasis is on making assumptions visible and keeping data, rules and interpretation apart.

This includes work with structured records, controlled fields, transformation checks, reproducible scripts, validation reports and audit trails. The strength is not that a small script proves a scientific claim. The strength is that the script makes the claim boundary inspectable.

## 3. Current Independent Research Work

My current work is independent research on QSB, treated here as a data-driven, relational and reproducible framework. The wider QSB programme is broader than the OUTREACH01A demonstrator, but it is not being submitted for assessment in this contact material.

I isolate one small question in the present package: how to represent two declared dynamically equivalent but non-identical records, their phase positions, their domains and an open boundary representation without turning that representation into a physical assertion.

My relevant strengths for this contact are specific: formal state descriptions, relational bookkeeping, statistical and computational modelling, reproducible runners, schema-like validation, careful separation of language views from canonical data, and defensive scientific writing.

## 6. Clear Boundaries

My background does not include established expertise in laser physics, an experimental DTC programme, institutional AG affiliation, or independent validation of QSB.
