# QSB External Data and Evidence Policy

- External raw data, third-party PDFs, and copied library/source files remain local and are not versioned in this repository.
- Versioned artifacts are limited to manifests, source/URL references, versions, retrieval dates, sizes, SHA-256 checksums, and self-authored evidence notes.
- Run outputs stay under `runs/` and are not committed.
- Retrieval and validation steps should be reproducible and documented from local evidence metadata.
- This repository does not transfer or claim licensing for external material.
