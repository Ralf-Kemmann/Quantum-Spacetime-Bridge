# Scientific Claim Boundaries

This document states the public claim discipline for the current Quantum–Spacetime Bridge repository.

## Five levels that must remain separate

### Finding

A value, relation, numerical outcome, exact derivation, validation result, or documented negative result.

### Interpretation

A bounded explanation that follows from the finding under the stated model assumptions.

### Hypothesis

A possible physical or mathematical explanation that remains to be tested.

### Open gap

A missing derivation, calibration, control, data source, or bridge.

### Claim boundary

A statement that the present evidence does not support and must not be implied.

## Currently supportable statements

The repository may state that:

- finite graph models with matter and edge variables have been defined and analysed;
- part of the current model admits exact mathematical treatment;
- the implemented edge response is bounded and has a unique stable stationary solution within the model;
- numerical campaigns and controls have been performed across independent graph realisations;
- small model-internal geometry-to-matter effects are directionally consistent across the evaluated seeds;
- the PostgreSQL DWH, metadata system, Data Marts, and hypercube provide provenance-controlled research infrastructure;
- negative, neutral, rejected, unresolved, and repair-required outcomes are retained.

Every such statement must remain tied to its model, data, run family, and validation scope.

## Statements not established

The repository must not claim that it has demonstrated:

- physical spacetime emergence;
- quantum gravity;
- a physical gravitational field;
- a physical metric or calibrated spacetime distance;
- Lorentz covariance;
- physical light cones or causal propagation;
- a universal de Broglie mechanism;
- a freely generated non-trivial ground state;
- a matter- or isotope-specific effect;
- an experimentally observed QSB signal;
- global uniqueness or universality outside the stated finite model;
- novelty priority for OLAP or hypercube methods without a dedicated literature review.

## Illustrations and scientific figures

Scientific figures are evidence-bearing only when they are generated from documented model outputs or source data.

Conceptual illustrations may be used as editorial eye-catchers and reading pauses. They must be labelled as illustrations and must not be described as simulations, observations, or physical predictions.

## Historical records

A later clarification does not erase an earlier preregistered decision. Both remain visible with their different roles.

A rejected candidate is not silently removed.

A result from one project strand is not imported into another strand merely because the language appears similar.

## Publication rule

The language may be as clear and confident as the evidence permits, but never stronger than the evidence.

For the current physics manuscript, the correct overall description is:

> an exploratory mathematical and computational study of a finite, dimensionless, coupled matter–edge-field graph model.
