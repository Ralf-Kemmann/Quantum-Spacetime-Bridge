# Publication Roadmap

## Principle

The public repository is prepared first. Later publications should point back to a transparent, versioned, and citable technical basis.

The scientific evidence remains the same across platforms. Language, depth, visual pacing, and submission format may differ.

## 1. GitHub public research surface

Purpose:

- explain the project in two minutes;
- provide current status and claim boundaries;
- make the research instrument visible;
- guide readers into the audit trail;
- prepare a citable release;
- keep code, data, figures, and methods connected.

This is the active step.

## 2. Formal physics manuscript

Planned venue profile: a conventional theoretical/computational physics journal.

Style:

- formal scientific English;
- LaTeX-oriented structure;
- model equations, exact results, numerical controls, tables, and evidence figures;
- minimal editorial illustration;
- concise infrastructure and AI-disclosure notes.

Scientific focus:

- finite coupled Schrödinger–edge-field graph dynamics;
- exact model structure;
- cross-seed robustness;
- constrained and free branches;
- time evolution and numerical adequacy;
- explicit physical limitations.

## 3. Academia presentation

Purpose:

- make the same research understandable to a broader scientifically interested audience;
- retain the author’s personal explanatory voice;
- use conceptual eye-catchers and visual reading pauses;
- distinguish illustrations from evidence-bearing figures.

Visual profile:

- editorial science-magazine pacing inspired by P.M., Spektrum / Scientific American, and a controlled touch of OMNI;
- dark backgrounds, blue/cyan structure, warm gold accents, and clear typographic hierarchy;
- no sensational claim inflation.

## 4. ESA Open Space Innovation Platform (OSIP)

Purpose:

- present novelty, present maturity, risk, possible value, and a testable development route;
- use a technically elegant visual language between the journal and magazine styles;
- distinguish the physical model route from the research-infrastructure / hypercube route.

OSIP material is not merely a shortened journal paper.

## 5. Hypercube and computational-physics methods paper

Central contribution:

- representation of the known research space as a metadata-governed n-dimensional hypercube;
- jointly addressable spectral, simulation, source, quality, control, repair, and claim states;
- formal Slice, Dice, Pivot, Roll-up, Drill-down, Drill-through, Drill-across, scenarios, and calculated measures;
- scientific grain and aggregation contracts;
- provenance-preserving analysis across Data Marts;
- preservation and analysis of negative and rejected research space.

Candidate fields include computational physics, scientific data engineering, research software, provenance, and reproducibility.

Novelty wording remains conservative until the dedicated literature review is complete.

## 6. Releases and DOI records

A repository release should be frozen before formal submission.

The release process should include:

- version tag;
- release notes;
- citation metadata;
- licence review;
- manuscript and figure mapping;
- reproducibility instructions;
- archived release record and DOI where applicable.

Publication DOI, repository-release DOI, Academia record, and OSIP identifiers are separate objects and must not be conflated.
